About Us

The people behind Abhieshwari Solar

A small, UPNEDA-registered team based in Gorakhpur, doing rooftop solar installation for homes and businesses across the city and nearby areas.

Who we are

M/s Abhieshwari Enterprises and Developers, trading as Abhieshwari Solar, is a rooftop solar installation business based in Gorakhnath, Gorakhpur. We install residential, shop, office and factory systems, and handle the PM Surya Ghar and UPNEDA subsidy paperwork, DISCOM coordination and net metering follow-up as part of the job, not as a separate charge.
