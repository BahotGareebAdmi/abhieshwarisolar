This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies only to this website and not to any offline interactions you may have with us (for example, a site visit or phone call).

Information we collect
Information you give us directly

Contact / site-visit form (Contact page): full name, phone number, email (optional), city or area, and any details you type about your requirement. This form is handled by our hosting provider, Netlify, and is delivered to us to schedule your free site visit.

Testimonial submissions (Testimonials page): your name, area/city, testimonial message, an optional photo, and an optional email address. Your name, area, message and photo are shown publicly only after we review and approve the submission. Your email address is never shown publicly and is used only if we need to contact you about your submission. This data is stored in our database, provided by Supabase.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Netlify) automatically logs standard technical information for every visit, such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and referring page, for security, abuse prevention and performance purposes. We do not use this data to personally identify individual visitors.

Local storage on your device

This site saves two small preferences directly in your browser's local storage (not sent to our servers): your light/dark theme choice and your English/Hindi language choice. These stay on your device and are used only to remember your preference the next time you visit. Clearing your browser data removes them.

How we use your information

We use the information above to: respond to your site-visit or quote request; contact you about scheduling and installation; assist with PM Surya Ghar / UPNEDA subsidy paperwork and net-metering applications where you've asked us to; review and, if approved, publish your testimonial; maintain accounting and GST records as required by Indian law; and improve this website. We do not use your contact-form or testimonial data for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Third-party services we use

We rely on a small number of trusted third-party services to run this website. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data:

  • Netlify, website hosting and the contact form.
  • Supabase, database and file storage for testimonials.
  • Google Fonts, loads typefaces used on this site; your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them.
  • Google AdSense, once active, may show ads on this site and use cookies to personalize them (see "Cookies & advertising" below).
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook, links on this site that open WhatsApp (Meta), Instagram (Meta) or Facebook (Meta) are governed by Meta's own privacy policy once you leave our site.
Cookies & advertising

This site itself does not set tracking cookies. If Google AdSense is active on this site, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info/choices. If we enable Google Analytics in future to understand site usage, it will use its own cookies; we will update this policy when that happens.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only: with government/DISCOM portals (such as the PM Surya Ghar national portal and UPNEDA) as part of arranging your subsidy and net-metering application, with your knowledge as part of that service; with the service providers listed above, solely to operate this website; or where required by law, such as tax and GST record-keeping.

Data retention

We keep contact-form and testimonial data for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, or as required by applicable tax and business record-keeping laws, after which it is deleted or anonymized. You can ask us to delete your testimonial or contact details at any time using the details below.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and general good practice, you can ask us to: tell you what personal information we hold about you; correct inaccurate information; delete your information (for example, remove a published testimonial or photo); or withdraw any consent you've given. To exercise any of these, email info@abhieshwarisolar.in or call +91 90265 73953, we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults arranging solar installations and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access-controlled databases. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example as our website or the services we use change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Significant changes will be reflected here.
