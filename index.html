FAQs

Common questions about rooftop solar

How much does solar panel installation cost in Gorakhpur?

Cost depends on system size, which we estimate from your roof area, monthly electricity bill and venue type. Use the solar calculator for an instant estimate, or book a free site visit for an exact quote.

What subsidy is available for rooftop solar in Uttar Pradesh?

Residential installations can combine the central PM Surya Ghar subsidy (up to ₹78,000) with the Uttar Pradesh state top-up via UPNEDA (up to ₹30,000) — a combined benefit of up to about ₹1,08,000, subject to current UPNEDA budget allocation.

Do you charge extra for handling the subsidy paperwork?

No. Portal registration, DISCOM coordination, net metering and subsidy claim follow-up are all included free as part of every installation.
