3kW Solar Se Kitni Bijli Bachegi? Tata vs Adani vs Loom Solar vs Waaree

Yeh woh sawaal hain jo humein phone aur WhatsApp par sabse zyada milte hain. Isliye seedhe, waise hi jaise aap poochte hain.

3 kW solar lagane se bijli bill me kitna fark padega?

Ek achhi tarah lagaya gaya 3kW system, average roughly 350-360 units har mahina generate karta hai (yeh dhoop, shading aur season ke hisaab se thoda upar-neeche hota hai). Ghar ke domestic tariff, roughly ₹7/unit maan kar, iska matlab hai lagbhag ₹2,400-2,500 mahina ki bill bachat, agar aapka consumption itna ya usse zyada hai.

System cost subsidy se pehle roughly ₹1,50,000-₹1,95,000 padta hai (3kW ke liye). Central PM Surya Ghar subsidy (up to ₹78,000) aur UP state UPNEDA top-up (up to ₹30,000) milakar, subsidy up to ₹1,08,000 tak ho sakti hai, isse net cost roughly ₹42,000-₹87,000 reh jaata hai. Iska matlab payback aksar 2-3 saal ke andar aa jaata hai, lekin exact number aapke roof, bill aur consumption pattern par depend karta hai. Apna number turant dekhne ke liye solar calculator use karein, yeh live, personalized estimate deta hai, guess nahi.

Bill aur units samajhne ke liye electricity bill aur payback guide dekhein, aur sahi size chunne ke liye system sizing guide padhein.

Konsa brand lagwayen: Tata, Adani, Loom Solar ya Waaree?

Yeh chaaron hi established, ALMM-listed (Approved List of Models and Manufacturers) brands hain jo India me widely use hote hain aur government subsidy ke liye eligible hain. Ek jaisi wattage ke panels me, bijli generation broadly similar hoti hai, chahe brand koi bhi ho, asli fark price, warranty terms aur us waqt available discount me hota hai, na ki generation me.

Kuch general points, jo current market patterns par based hain (exact terms model ke hisaab se badalte hain, quote ke time confirm karte hain):

  • Tata (Tata Power Solar): bade group ka strong brand backing, typically ~25-year performance aur ~10-year product warranty ke range me. Premium-positioned brand hai, isliye dealer margin aur discount room usually sabse kam hota hai.
  • Adani Solar: large-scale, vertically integrated manufacturer, typically ~25-year performance aur ~12-year product warranty ke range me. Competitive pricing, aur filhal humein isme better discount mil raha hai.
  • Waaree: India ka sabse bada module manufacturer by capacity, wide service network. Warranty terms generally strong hote hain, pricing competitive rehti hai, discount room Adani/Loom Solar se thoda kam.
  • Loom Solar: Faridabad-based Indian manufacturer, panels ke saath inverters aur lithium batteries bhi banata hai, PM Surya Ghar portal par pan-India empanelled hai. Typically ~28-year performance warranty ke range me. Filhal humein isme sabse achha discount mil raha hai.

Hum yeh saare four brands install karte hain, aur honestly bataayein to: filhal Loom Solar aur Adani par sabse zyada discount available hai, Waaree par thoda kam, aur Tata par sabse kam margin kyunki wo premium-priced brand hai. Yeh dealer-level pricing/promotions time ke saath badalti rehti hai, isliye humse seedha baat karke apne budget ke hisaab se exact aaj ka quote lein.

Kis brand ya system me kharcha kam aur bijli zyada bachegi?

Yeh do alag sawaal hain, aur inhe milana confusion create karta hai. Bijli ki bachat mainly system ki sahi sizing par depend karti hai, aapke roof aur bill se match karta hua size, na ki brand se. Kharcha kam karna teen cheezon ka combination hai: sahi size (na zyada bada, na chhota), poora subsidy claim karna, aur us waqt jis brand par best discount mil raha ho usse choose karna. Sabse better value in teeno ko saath me optimize karne se milti hai, sirf brand badalne se nahi.

Bill me kitna "off" milega solar lagane ke baad?

Technically yeh "off" ya discount nahi hai, yeh aapki apni generate ki hui bijli hai jo net metering ke through aapke bill ko offset karti hai. Sahi size ka system lagne par bill lagbhag fixed charges tak aa sakta hai. Subsidy ek alag cheez hai, wo aapke installation ke upfront cost ko kam karti hai (up to ₹1,08,000), yeh bill se seedha nahi juda, par overall cost bahut kam kar deta hai.
