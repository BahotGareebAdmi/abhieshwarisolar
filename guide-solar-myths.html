Common Solar Myths & Mistakes: Busted

Seven things we hear often from customers in Gorakhpur, and the actual, straightforward answer to each.

Myth: Solar doesn't work well in Gorakhpur's monsoon or cloudy weather

Fact: Panels generate less on cloudy or overcast days, not zero, output reduces, it doesn't stop. Monsoon months naturally produce less than peak summer, but a properly sized system is planned around your full-year average, not just the sunniest days. Rain is also, incidentally, a free panel wash. See our how solar works guide for the full explanation.

Myth: You need a battery for rooftop solar to work

Fact: Most homes use a grid-connected (on-grid) setup with net metering, surplus power is exported to the grid and drawn back later, rather than stored in a battery on-site. A battery is an optional add-on for backup during outages, not a requirement for solar itself to function or save money.

Myth: The subsidy covers the full installation cost

Fact: The combined central and UP state subsidy meaningfully reduces cost, up to roughly ₹1,08,000 for a 3kW-or-larger residential system, but it's a partial offset, not full coverage. See our cost breakdown guide for realistic total numbers.

Myth: Any local electrician can install it and you'll still get the subsidy

Fact: Subsidy eligibility requires installation by a vendor on the official state-wise empanelled list. A technically fine installation by a non-empanelled electrician can still disqualify your subsidy claim entirely. This is one of the most costly mistakes to make. Always verify empanelment first; see our installer selection guide.

Myth: Bigger is always better

Fact: Oversizing a system relative to actual consumption increases upfront cost without a proportional increase in savings. Correct sizing depends on roof area, electricity bill and consumption pattern, our sizing guide covers how this is actually calculated.

Myth: A pricier panel brand generates noticeably more electricity

Fact: Among ALMM-listed brands at the same wattage, generation is broadly similar, the real differences are price, warranty terms and current dealer discount, not how much sunlight gets turned into units. See our Tata vs Adani vs Loom Solar vs Waaree comparison for the honest breakdown.

Myth: Solar panels need constant, expensive maintenance

Fact: Panels have no moving parts and are genuinely low-maintenance, periodic cleaning (roughly every 2–3 weeks in dusty months) and the occasional visual check is typically all that's needed. Full details in our maintenance guide.

Myth: Solar is only worth it for homeowners, not businesses

Fact: Commercial connections don't get the residential subsidy, but higher commercial tariffs and accelerated depreciation tax benefits often make payback for shops, offices and factories just as strong, sometimes faster. See our commercial ROI guide.
