How Much Does Solar Panel Installation Cost in Gorakhpur?

A realistic breakdown of pricing, what moves the number up or down, and how the subsidy fits in.

Cost is usually the first question, and the honest answer is "it depends", but not in a vague way. A handful of specific factors determine the final number, and once you know them, you can get a genuinely accurate estimate rather than a guess. This guide covers typical price ranges, what actually drives cost up or down, and where the subsidy fits into the payment timeline.

Typical price range before subsidy

As a general market range in India, residential rooftop solar typically costs somewhere between ₹45,000 and ₹65,000 per kW before subsidy, depending on components and site conditions. That means, roughly:

  • A 1kW system: roughly ₹45,000–₹65,000
  • A 3kW system (common for a mid-size home): roughly ₹1,35,000–₹1,95,000
  • A 5kW system (larger home or small commercial): roughly ₹2,25,000–₹3,25,000

These are general ranges to plan around, not a fixed quote, treat them as a starting point. Your exact number depends on the factors below, and the only way to get a precise figure is our solar calculator or a free on-site assessment.

What actually moves the price

  • Panel and inverter brand/tier. ALMM-listed panels (required for subsidy eligibility) and reputable inverter brands cost more than unbranded alternatives, but generally last longer and perform more consistently over 25 years. See our Tata vs Adani vs Loom Solar vs Waaree comparison for how brand affects price.
  • Roof type and mounting structure. A flat RCC roof with clear access is the simplest and cheapest to mount on. Sloped roofs, tin sheds, or roofs needing structural reinforcement or elevated stands add to the cost.
  • System size. Cost per kW usually drops slightly as system size increases, since fixed costs (labour, wiring, net meter application) are spread across more capacity.
  • Distance from main panel to meter/DISCOM connection point. Longer cable runs and more complex wiring add material and labour cost.
  • Shading and layout complexity. A roof with chimneys, water tanks or partial shading may need micro-inverters or optimisers to avoid one shaded panel dragging down the whole string, this adds cost but protects overall output.

How the subsidy affects your actual payment

It's important to understand the timing: the subsidy is disbursed after installation and commissioning, credited directly to your bank account, it does not reduce what you pay the installer upfront. For a 3kW residential system, the combined central and UP state subsidy can bring the effective cost down by up to roughly ₹1,08,000, but that money arrives a few weeks after commissioning, not before. See our full subsidy guide for exact eligibility and amounts, or our solar loan & EMI guide if you'd rather bridge that upfront cost with financing.

Commercial and factory installations

Shops, offices and factories aren't eligible for the residential subsidy, but the economics work differently and are often even more favourable, commercial electricity tariffs are typically higher than residential ones, which shortens the payback period, and businesses can claim accelerated depreciation as a tax benefit. We cover this in our commercial solar ROI guide.
